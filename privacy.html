WHO-KNOCKS Drive Sync

Privacy notice

Personal, owner-operated desktop access to Google Drive.

Data used

The desktop application uses Google account identity and authorized Drive metadata to verify the intended account and storage locations. It reads file names, folder structure, identifiers, sizes, timestamps, checksums and file contents as needed for browsing, recovery and approved backups. Shared Drive access depends on the signed-in account's existing membership.

OAuth credentials are stored in protected local configuration files. File caches, transfer receipts, manifests and recovery copies are also stored locally. File-system permissions limit access, but they do not themselves provide disk encryption.

Transfers and sharing

Authorized file transfers take place between the owner's computer and Google Drive. Uploaded files inherit the access rules of the selected Drive destination, including Shared Drive membership. The application does not add members or make files public as part of a backup.

The desktop helper does not send Drive data to an advertising service, analytics service or AI provider. AI tools and connected assistant services are separate products with their own permissions; they are not automatically authorized by signing into this desktop application.

Google's services process data under their applicable terms and privacy policies. This static information site contains no forms, third-party scripts, analytics or remote fonts. Its hosting provider may process ordinary web-request metadata under that provider's policy.

Retention and deletion

Local caches have configured resource limits. Recovery originals and versioned backup files are retained until the owner deliberately removes them; there is no automatic retention-pruning promise. Disabling a job or revoking Google access stops future authorized operations but does not delete existing local or cloud copies.

Initial recovery is non-destructive. Ongoing backup behavior must be reviewed before activation. A matching checksum verifies a specific copy, not the completeness of every possible recovery source.

Your controls

The owner can stop local mounts and jobs, inspect or remove local configuration, and revoke the application's Google connection in Google Account connections. Remove retained files separately if deletion is intended. Revocation does not delete files from Drive.

For questions about this personal application, use the support contact shown on its Google consent screen. This notice describes the local desktop application; it does not replace policies of Google or independently connected tools.
