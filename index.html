What the application does

This personal Linux desktop setup uses rclone and local helpers to browse authorized Google Drive storage, recover existing documents, and prepare controlled backups of selected local documents and projects.

It is operated on the account owner's computer. This information site does not run a sync service, request Google sign-in, or receive documents or credentials.

Why Drive access is requested

Reading existing files enables browsing and recovery. Writing access enables approved backups into the owner's chosen My Drive folders and Shared Drives. The Google Drive permission can cover more files than the locally configured jobs; those local path restrictions are application safeguards, not a restriction enforced by the OAuth scope.

Grant access only to the expected desktop client through Google's consent screen. This site never asks you to paste a password, authorization code, token, or service-account key.

Control stays with the owner

Local browsing views and backup jobs have separate controls. Initial recovery, credential acceptance and upload activation are separate steps. An installed application does not mean automatic uploads are enabled.

Review the privacy notice before authorizing the desktop application. Support contact information is provided on its Google consent screen.
